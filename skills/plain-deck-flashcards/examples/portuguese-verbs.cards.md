# Portuguese verbs (Brazil)

Recognizing a word and producing it are different skills, so each verb has two cards, one in each
direction. Example sentences go in the answer, where they add context without giving anything away.

## What does "decorar" mean in Brazilian Portuguese?
To memorize, to learn by heart: "Vou decorar a matéria para a prova."

It also means "to decorate"; context tells which.

## How do you say "to memorize" in Brazilian Portuguese?
**decorar** (also *memorizar*).

## What does "esquecer" mean?
To forget: "Não esqueça a chave!" (Don't forget the key!)

## How do you say "to forget" in Portuguese?
**esquecer**: "Esqueci o nome dele." (I forgot his name.)

## Which verb fills the gap in "Eu ___ café todo dia" (I drink coffee every day)?
**tomo**, from *tomar*: in Brazil you *tomar* coffee, water or juice. *Beber* is also correct; *tomar* is
the everyday choice.

## What is the past tense (pretérito perfeito) of "eu faço"?
**eu fiz** (I did, I made). It is irregular: *fazer* → *fiz, fez, fizemos, fizeram*.
